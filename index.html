B(a). Maritime Narrative Analytics

Responsible AI Institute funding

Breaks each report into a narrative structure (orientation, complicating action, harm, resolution), profiles of the vessels, people, places and equipment in it, and a chain of events, all published as an open ontology. Adapts Steve’s Hostile Narrative Analysis (Anning, 2023), and every extracted fact traces back to its sentence and the grammar rule that produced it. Feeds pattern finding and proposed coding (section B), and overlaps with event sequences in section E.

Appendix: Copper (£50k)

An alternative to Steel. Instead of building our own chat, we build a CHIRPdb MCP server: a connector that users add to Claude. Claude provides the chat screen, memory, history, charts, uploads and mobile apps; our server provides the verified reports, search and counting across every report. Most of section A comes with Claude, so the budget goes into the backend.

What Copper trades away
  • Users need a Claude account and must add the connector. Which Claude plans and apps support custom connectors needs confirming before we commit.
  • Claude writes the answers, not us. Our tools return only verified records with citations, and our instructions tell Claude to cite every claim and refuse off-topic questions, but we cannot stop it drawing on its own knowledge or web search.
  • Documents users upload go to Anthropic under the user’s Claude terms, not deleted by CHIRP at session end.
  • The experience is Claude’s, not CHIRP’s branded product.
  • Question-answering costs move to the user’s Claude plan. CHIRP pays only for hosting and search.
